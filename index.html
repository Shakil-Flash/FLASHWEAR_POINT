EDITORIAL VISION

Art Direction Principles

Campaign & Product Framing
Model Direction
Self-possessed, detached, sculptural stillness. No smiling. Poses emphasize garment silhouette, shoulder drop, and fabric geometry.
Lighting System
Directional hard key-light with razor-edged cast shadows against brutalist concrete or deep velvet studio black falloff.
Aspect Ratios
16:9 for desktop hero cinematics, 4:5 for mobile editorial lookbooks, 3:4 for e-commerce product cards, 1:1 for grid thumbnails.
Restraint Mandate
Electric Volt (#D4FF00) strictly restricted to ≤2% screen area. No aggressive sales popups or discount ribbons.
